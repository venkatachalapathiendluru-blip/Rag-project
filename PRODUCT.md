# Product context

Written from the project brief, without a design interview. Assumptions are marked.

- **Product:** Enterprise RAG Platform. It answers questions about uploaded documents and shows the evidence behind each answer.
- **Users (assumption):** an AI engineer running and evaluating the system, and technical reviewers or hiring managers looking at a demo.
- **Use scene (assumption):** a desk, a long working session, reading answers and checking sources. People switch between chat and report tables.
- **Mode:** Operate. The tool should disappear into the task.
- **Tone:** exact and calm. It states what is known, and says plainly when something was not measured or not approved.
- **Must never do:** invent metrics, show an unavailable value as zero, simulate streaming, or merge evaluation versions.
- **Brief's direction:** clean enterprise dashboard, consistent spacing, professional type, responsive sidebar, light and dark themes. No excess animation, gradients, or fake charts.
